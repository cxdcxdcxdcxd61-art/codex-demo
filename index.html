01 · About

Photographer Profile

A quiet eye for light, texture, and human presence.

Adrien Vale is the founder of LENS ATELIER, a high-end photography practice working across intimate weddings, editorial portraiture, fashion campaigns, and collectible fine art imagery.

With more than a decade behind the camera, the studio has developed a restrained visual language: soft contrast, sculptural shadows, deliberate negative space, and images that feel both cinematic and deeply personal.

Experience 12+ years

Editorial, commercial, and private commissions across Europe, Asia, and North America.

Philosophy Less, but unforgettable.

Every frame is composed to remove noise and preserve the emotion, architecture, and atmosphere of a moment.
